# Marketing Funnel & Conversion Performance Analysis

## Objective

Analyze customer responses and identify conversion opportunities using marketing campaign data. The project focuses on understanding customer behavior, measuring conversion performance, and providing recommendations to improve future marketing campaigns.

## Tools Used

* Power BI
* Microsoft Excel

## KPIs

* Total Customers: 2240
* Total Responses: 334
* Response Rate: 14.91%

## Key Insights

1. A total of 2,240 customers were analyzed in the marketing campaign.
2. The overall customer response rate was 14.91%.
3. Graduation-level customers showed higher response rates compared to other education groups.
4. Customers with higher website engagement were more likely to respond.
5. A large portion of customers did not respond, indicating opportunities for conversion improvement.

## Recommendations

1. Focus marketing efforts on high-response customer segments.
2. Increase campaign personalization based on customer demographics.
3. Improve customer engagement through digital channels.
4. Retarget customers who did not respond to previous campaigns.
5. Use successful campaign strategies to optimize future marketing efforts.

## Conclusion

The analysis revealed that customer engagement and demographic factors significantly influence campaign responses. By improving targeting, personalization, and engagement strategies, businesses can enhance conversion rates and overall marketing performance.
