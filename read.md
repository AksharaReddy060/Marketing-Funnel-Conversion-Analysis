**Marketing Funnel \& Conversion Performance Analysis Project Overview**



This project focuses on analyzing marketing campaign performance and customer conversion behavior using Power BI. The objective is to identify customer response patterns, evaluate conversion rates, analyze drop-off points, and provide actionable recommendations to improve marketing effectiveness.



Objectives

Analyze customer response and conversion performance.

Identify customer segments with higher engagement.

Evaluate the impact of website activity on customer responses.

Generate business insights and recommendations for future campaigns.

Tools Used

Power BI

Microsoft Excel

Dataset



Marketing Campaign Dataset containing customer demographics, campaign responses, website visits, and purchasing behavior.



Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

Total Customers: 2,240

Total Responses: 334

Response Rate: 14.91%

Response Distribution Pie Chart

Key Insights and Recommendations

Key Insights

A total of 2,240 customers were analyzed.

The overall response rate was 14.91%.

Graduation-level customers showed higher response rates.

Higher website engagement was associated with better response performance.

A large percentage of customers did not respond, indicating conversion improvement opportunities.

Recommendations

Focus marketing efforts on high-response customer segments.

Increase campaign personalization.

Improve customer engagement through digital channels.

Retarget non-responding customers.

Optimize future campaigns using successful marketing strategies.

Conclusion



The analysis highlights important customer behavior trends and conversion opportunities. By improving targeting, personalization, and customer engagement strategies, businesses can enhance campaign performance and increase conversion rates.
